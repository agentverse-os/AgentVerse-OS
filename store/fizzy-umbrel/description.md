Fizzy is a fresh, modern, and delightfully simple Kanban board from 37signals (the makers of Basecamp and HEY).

Tired of bloated tools like Trello or Jira? Fizzy strips away the unnecessary features and delivers a fast, fun, and intuitive way to track tasks, bugs, ideas, issues, or small projects. With its colorful UI, smooth drag-and-drop boards and auto-closing for inactive cards, it keeps your workflow light and focused.

Perfect for personal use, small teams, or anyone running on umbrelOS who wants privacy-first project management without the bloat — Kanban as it should be.
