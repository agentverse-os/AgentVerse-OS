Downtify allows you to download music by copy-pasting the Spotify link for a song, album, etc. The songs are downloaded from YouTube, along with album art, lyrics, and other metadata about the songs. As soon as your downloads are complete you will be notified!

Users are responsible for their actions and potential legal consequences. We do not support unauthorized downloading of copyrighted material and take no responsibility for user actions.
